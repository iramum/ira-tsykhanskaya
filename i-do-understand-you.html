I Do Understand You But Cannot Speak

Year
2020–2021
Medium
Non-VR game, plasma screens, laser-engraved acrylic, epoxy floor
With
ShShSh collective

A game broadcast to two horizontal plasma screens. Only one user can interact with the virtual space at a time; the others are given the expectant role of an observer.

Text

The project consists of a non-VR game that is broadcast to two horizontal plasmas with different heights for an external viewer. It was important for us that only one user can interact with the virtual space at a time. Others were given the expectant role of an observer.

The third screen is vertically oriented in space and is dedicated to the glitter pool, the unspoken symbol of our community. In addition, the floor here acts as an RPG map of the house. It is made of a toneless layer of epoxy resin and a banner. On one of the walls there are cards of the artists' characters, made by laser engraving on plexiglass. Each character has a symbol, motto and image.
