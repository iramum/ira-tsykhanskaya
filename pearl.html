Pearl

Year
2018–2019
Medium
Interdisciplinary project
With
with Anastasia Alekhina

A future in which human beings may want to control the colour of their biological fluids and bones. Artists and biologists develop a pearlescent dye based on aragonite crystals.

Text

Project Pearl is an interdisciplinary study whose goal is to work with issues of human identity and physicality. We made the assumption that in the near future human beings will want to control the colour of their biological fluids and/or bones, for example, to make ejaculate or pearlescent teeth. In the joint work of artists and biologists, we want to develop a pearlescent dye based on aragonite crystals.
